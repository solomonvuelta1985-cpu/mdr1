Technical Notes - Affected Population

Affected Barangays (Brgys)
Indicate either the names (for city/municipal and provincial level) or number (for regional level) of barangays affected within a city or municipality.

Affected Families and Persons
Report the number of affected families and persons. Data should be provided as both cumulative and current.

No. of Evacuation Centers (ECs)
Indicate the number of evacuation centers that have been opened or are currently operational.

Inside ECs
Report the number of displaced families and individuals currently staying inside evacuation centers. Disaggregate into cumulative and current.

Outside ECs
Report the number of displaced families and individuals staying outside evacuation centers, such as with relatives or friends. Disaggregate into cumulative and current.

Total Displaced
Sum of displaced families and individuals inside and outside evacuation centers. Provide both cumulative and current figures.

Cumulative
Refers to the total count accumulated from the start of the incident or reporting period up to the present date.

Current
Refers to the latest available figures for a specific date or reporting period (e.g., current day, reporting week).
