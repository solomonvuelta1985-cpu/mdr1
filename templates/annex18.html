Technical Notes - Pre-emptive Evacuation (Animals)
General Guide: Pre-emptive evacuation of animals is crucial to safeguard livelihoods, prevent economic losses, and reduce risks to both humans and animals during disasters. This table captures animals evacuated ahead of anticipated hazard impacts.

Region, Province, City/Municipality, Barangay
Indicate the specific geographic location where pre-emptive animal evacuation occurred.

Classification
Identify whether the animals fall under:

  • Livestock
  • Poultry

Type
Provide the specific animal type based on the classification:

  • Livestock:
    • Pig
    • Cow
    • Goat
    • Horse
  • Poultry:
    • Chicken
    • Duck

Quantity
Indicate the total number of evacuated animals by type. Use whole numbers only.

Reason for Evacuation
Specify why the animals were evacuated (e.g., expected flooding, volcanic eruption, typhoon).

Temporary Shelter Location
Indicate where the animals are being temporarily housed (e.g., designated evacuation pens, community centers).

Assistance Provider
Identify who is providing assistance for the evacuation (e.g., LGU, DA, NGOs).

Remarks
Include any relevant details such as:

  • Assistance or coordination needs from LGU, DA, or NGOs
  • Gaps in resources or support
  • Special requirements for specific animal types
  • Expected duration of evacuation
