Technical Notes — Annex 17

Region / Province / City / Barangay: Indicate the exact geographic location of the evacuated population.

Sex and Age Distribution: Provide counts by sex and age groups. Each group has Cumulative and Current values. Cumulative refers to total since start of incident/reporting period; Current refers to latest figures for a date or reporting week.

Vulnerable groups definitions
  • Pregnant Women — Women currently expecting a child.
  • Lactating Mothers — Women who are breastfeeding.
  • Child-Headed Family — Household headed by a minor (below 18 y.o.).
  • Single-Headed Family — An adult who is sole breadwinner without spouse/children.
  • Solo Parent — Defined under RA 8972 and RA 11861 as sole responsible parent under similar circumstances.
  • Persons with Disability (PWDs) — Individuals with permanent or temporary impairments.
  • Indigenous Peoples (IPs) — Members of recognized indigenous cultural communities or tribes.
  • 4Ps Beneficiaries — Individuals/families registered under the Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program.

Remarks: include areas with highest displacement, conditions in evacuation centers, special concerns (medical, lack of supplies), coordination or assistance needed/received.

Totals: "Cumulative" refers to totals accumulated from the start of incident or reporting period up to present date. "Current" refers to latest available figures for a specific date or reporting period.
