Technical Notes
General Guidelines: The table for related incidents is dedicated for reporting secondary incidents or hazards caused by the primary incidents or hazards being monitored. A fire incident following an earthquake or flooding resulting from a weather disturbance fall into this category. The primary hazard event or incident should not be included in this table.

Region, Province, City/Municipality, Barangay
Indicate the specific location where the related incident occurred.

Type of Incident
Categorize the incident using standard terms such as landslide, flooding, fire, earthquake, etc. This allows for quick grouping and identification of similar incidents.

Date and Time
Indicate the exact date and time the incident occurred or was observed.

Description
Provide a brief summary of the incident.

Example: "The landslide incident affected four residential houses."

Actions Taken
Specify the response actions initiated by the concerned agencies or LGUs.

Example: "The LDRRMO deployed emergency response teams to the affected area."

Remarks
Include any other relevant details or contextual information about the incident.
