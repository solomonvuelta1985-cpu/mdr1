Technical Notes - Families Assisted
General Guide: Captures the number of families that require humanitarian or emergency assistance during or after a disaster, and the actual number served. The data provides insight into response gaps and helps guide further support or interventions.

Region, Province, City/Municipality, Barangay
Indicate the exact geographic location where the assistance is being monitored.

No. of Families Requiring Assistance
Reflects the total number of families that were identified as needing any form of assistance (e.g., food, non-food items, shelter, medical aid).

No. of Families Assisted
Total number of families who have already received assistance (partial or complete).

% of Families Assisted
This is the proportion of assisted families versus those requiring help.
Formula: (No. of Families Assisted ÷ No. of Families Requiring Assistance) × 100

Type of Assistance
Categorize the assistance provided using standard terms such as food packs, hygiene kits, shelter materials, etc.

Assistance Provider
Indicate the source or provider of assistance (e.g., LGU, DSWD, NGOs).

Remarks
Include additional information such as:

  • Type of assistance provided
  • Reasons for any gaps (e.g., inaccessible areas, supply constraints)
  • Target timeline for full coverage
