Technical Notes - Suspension of Classes

Region, Province, City/Municipality, Barangay
Specify the exact geographic area where class suspension was declared.

Level
Indicate the educational level affected by the suspension, such as:

  • Preschool / Kindergarten
  • Elementary
  • Secondary / High School
  • Senior High School
  • Tertiary / College / University
  • All Levels

Type
Indicate whether the suspension applies to:

  • Public - Government-run schools only
  • Private - Private learning institutions only
  • All - Both public and private institutions

Date / Time of Suspension
Specify when classes were officially suspended.

Date / Time of Resumption
Indicate when classes resumed, if applicable.

Remarks
Provide any relevant details, such as:

  • Reason for the suspension (e.g., typhoon signal, earthquake, transport strike, flooding)
  • Authority or issuing body (e.g., LGU Executive Order, DepEd Advisory, CHED Memorandum)
  • Scope of suspension (e.g., specific levels only, whole-day or half-day)
  • Special instructions (e.g., shift to modular/online delivery, school opening delay)
