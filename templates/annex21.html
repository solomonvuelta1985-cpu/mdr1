Technical Notes
General Guide: Tracks the provision of food and non-food items (F/NFIs) and other forms of assistance delivered to local government units (LGUs) and agencies to support their relief operations during or after a disaster.

Region, Province, City/Municipality
Specify the location where the recipient LGU or agency is based or where the assistance was delivered.

Recipient (LGU/Agency)
Include the specific name of the recipient (e.g., Municipality of Nabunturan, OCD Regional Office, PNP).

Cluster
Identify the humanitarian or sectoral cluster (e.g., Food, WASH, Shelter, Health, Protection).

Type
Specify the item or service provided, such as:

  • Food packs
  • Hygiene kits
  • Drinking water
  • Sleeping kits
  • Medical supplies
  • Shelter repair kits

Quantity
Number of items or service units distributed.

Unit
Measurement unit (e.g., pack, piece, liter, kilogram).

Cost per Unit
Estimated or actual cost of each unit.

Total Amount
Total cost of the assistance provided.
Formula: Quantity × Cost per Unit

Source
Identify the origin of the assistance (e.g., DSWD, DOH, LGU, NGO, private sector, foreign aid).

Remarks
Include relevant details about the assistance provided.
